# NBA Cup group-stage totals registration

This diagnostic tests whether the 2025 NBA Cup group-stage over probabilities
leave a positive calibration residual relative to ordinary games in the same
calendar weeks. Scoring tiebreaks motivate the question. They cannot isolate
a causal effect from schedule, team or rest differences.

The protocol freezes the date window, exact fixture crosswalk, coverage gates,
endpoint, weekly comparison and uncertainty before inspecting Cup outcomes.
The source season was previously used for other NBA research. Published
closing consensus is a development benchmark. The next required evidence
is the authoritative 60-game group schedule and season-specific rules.
No quote capture or statistical experiment was run for this registration.

# Soccer congestion theory: source-use audit — 2026-11-01

Status: proposed, not registered or tested. A plausible mechanism is that
congestion changes attacking output after confirmed rotation. The competing
explanation is that market prices already account for it; fatigue in scores
does not establish value at offered odds. No match rows or outcomes were
downloaded or evaluated in this audit.

Football-Data's own data page was checked directly:
https://football-data.co.uk/data.php

It advertises historical results/odds but limits intended use to private
individuals and explicitly restricts commercial or data-training products
using automated bots/scrapers/AI. Public availability and a free price do not
establish permission for this agent to download rows, train a model, redistribute
data or feed the phone app. Do not automate CSV acquisition from this source
unless documented permission covers the intended use. No account, purchase,
scraping workaround or paid API was used.

The publisher distinguishes pre-closing prices from C-labeled closing prices.
A column suffix alone is not an exact decision-time quote timestamp. The field
dictionary is at https://www.football-data.co.uk/notes.txt . Neither aggregated
maximum/average odds nor a foreign-market bookmaker column establishes an
available exact eligible-book execution price.

## Next permitted test

Use an independently authorized export/source with exact fixture identity,
actual schedule/recovery times, timestamped lineup announcements and revisions,
lagged minutes, competition/venue/travel, paired book prices and90-minute
settlement semantics. Freeze the chronological split and one primary
specification before viewing outcomes; preserve failures and account for team,
fixture and competition dependence.

Reject use of final target-game lineups, minutes or match statistics as earlier
features. Report missing lineup timing and fixture coverage; do not infer rest
from row order or replace missing domestic/European fixtures with a convenient
subset. Keep any closing-only descriptive benchmark separate from a
lineup-announcement decision test. Original methodology here contains no
licensed rows, statistical result or claim of an edge. NFL/NBA delivery priority
and existing frozen models are unchanged.

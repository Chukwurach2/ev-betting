# Reference sensitivity on phone cards

NFL and NBA cards compare the same offered price with each median formed by
omitting one canonical reference. Positive baseline estimates that become
nonpositive under any omission appear as reference-sensitive leans. The card
keeps its original estimated EV and rank and displays the omission EV range
in its details. Nonpositive baseline estimates remain price watches.

The omission range describes dependence on the available quotes. It carries
no statistical confidence or source-independence claim. Eligibility, offer
and reference age limits, paper records and the frozen model are unaffected.

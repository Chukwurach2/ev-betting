# Sensitivity of retained phone prices to reference books

On November 11 the fixed-offer audit rebuilt the median fair probability for
every paper observation with a retained reference list. It excluded lists with
the offered operator, repeated canonical operators or fewer than two references,
and required agreement with the stored median to within 0.000000000001. All
518 available lists passed. Another 54 legacy NBA observations lack lists.

For each passing observation the audit removed each reference operator once,
recomputed the median and compared the result with the same offered price.
A change means a switch between positive and nonpositive estimated EV at zero
additional fees. Repeated snapshots and related selections can share games.

| Sport | Positive under all omissions | Positive baseline, sensitive | Nonpositive baseline, sensitive | Nonpositive under all omissions |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| NFL | 0 | 0 | 0 | 30 |
| NBA | 1 | 4 | 6 | 477 |

Four of the five archived positive NBA estimates depended on keeping at least
one particular reference. The remaining positive observation survived this
specific sensitivity check. These are archived quote observations. The check
cannot verify execution, rule equivalence, source independence or performance.
No scores were used, and the ledger and its settlement records were preserved.

# NFL Edge promotion gate: SHADOW → candidate

Status: **contract**. Any proposal to advance a strategy out of SHADOW is judged
against these criteria, in full, with no exceptions. Meeting the gate qualifies
a strategy for a *paper-trading proposal only* — never real money. Real-money
consideration requires a separate, explicit user decision after paper trading.

"Actual closing lines" below means real observed provider quotes: settle.py's
`closing_consensus` — the median no-vig fair probability across books from the
latest pre-kickoff quotes **at the pick's exact line** (≥2 books) — computed
from actual closing snapshots, not modeled or synthetic prices. Consensus
proxies (model-implied closes, stale snapshots) do not count.

## The gate (all must hold simultaneously)

1. **Sample size.** ≥ 200 settled forward shadow picks, all generated by the
   live pipeline (declared engine version; any engine-version change restarts
   the count). Picks must come from real forward operation — backfilled or
   reconstructed picks do not count.

2. **Positive CLV vs actual closing lines.** Mean `clv_prob_points`
   (closing consensus fair prob − taken price fair prob, both de-vigged, at
   the pick's exact line) > 0 with a one-sided t-test p < 0.01. If multiple
   engine versions or markets were evaluated during the window, Holm
   correction applies across all of them.

3. **Practical effect size.** Mean CLV ≥ +1.0 probability point. A
   statistically significant but tiny edge does not pass — the bar is set
   before seeing the data and is not negotiable downward afterward.

4. **Calibration.** Binned calibration on settled picks: in every bucket with
   n ≥ 30, |actual win rate − mean predicted fair prob| ≤ 0.05. No bucket may
   be systematically overconfident (actual < predicted − 0.05).

5. **Time window.** The 200 picks must span ≥ 8 weeks of live season. A hot
   fortnight does not pass.

6. **No tuning on forward data.** Engine thresholds (MIN_EDGE, freshness,
   consensus rules, risk caps) are frozen for the entire evaluation window.
   Any threshold change restarts the window from zero. The gate's own
   parameters (200, 0.01, 1.0pp, 8 weeks) were fixed in this document before
   any forward evaluation and cannot be re-fit to make a result pass.

7. **Execution realism.** Picks must be generatable at quote time under the
   live freshness rule (quotes ≤ 30 min old at generation). The evaluated
   book's own quote is excluded from its consensus (strict LOBO) — a strategy
   may never take an edge against itself.

8. **Independent accounting.** CLV and results are computed by the settlement
   path (ops/settle.py), not by the strategy under evaluation. The strategy
   never scores itself.

## What passing means — and does not mean

- Passing qualifies the strategy for a **paper-trading proposal**: stakes
  recorded, no money risked, for a second window (≥ 4 weeks) before any
  real-money discussion.
- Passing is about the *strategy + execution loop*, not a single model
  artifact. A retrained or modified model is a new candidate.
- Historical backtests, no matter how strong, can never satisfy this gate.
  Only forward shadow evidence counts.
- "No bet" remains the standing position until this gate is met in full.

## Amendments

### 2026-09-12 — CLV definition corrected (consensus−close → close−taken)

The original contract defined CLV as pick-time consensus fair prob minus
closing fair prob. Audit found this has the wrong sign in the canonical
slow-book scenario and is perverse in general: when the market moves in favor
of the selection after the pick (the bet was good), consensus−close goes
negative; when the market proves the pick-time consensus wrong in the bet's
favor, it goes positive. It measures consensus drift, not the price advantage
of the taken bet.

Corrected definition (applied in ops/settle.py, migration 011): CLV =
closing consensus fair prob − taken price's de-vigged fair prob, at the
identical line. Positive means the pick beat the closing line. This matches
the industry-standard favorable-price convention the gate's "positive forward
CLV against actual observed closing lines" requirement intends. The taken
price's fair prob is stored on the pick at generation time (`taken_fair_prob`).
No gate parameters (200, 0.01, 1.0pp, 8 weeks) were changed — only the metric
was corrected to measure what it claims to measure. No forward picks existed
under the old definition, so no data was reinterpreted.

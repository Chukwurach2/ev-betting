# Discovered Hypothesis — FBS-vs-FCS Home Cover Lean

**Status:** DISCOVERED (exploratory). NOT confirmatory evidence.
**Discovered:** 2026-09-15, in F2 run 35004257658.
**Must be preregistered and tested on uninspected evidence before any claim.**

## Observation

In F2 (2022–2024, CFBD sign oracle), home teams covered the spread at
**55.6%** in FBS-vs-FCS games (n=942 event-windows), vs 50.2% in FBS-vs-FBS
(n=10,677). Nominal z ≈ 3.5σ vs 50%.

## Why this is not evidence

The hypothesis was formed AFTER seeing the result. The 942 observations
that generated it cannot confirm it (data dredging / HARKing). Per the
research contract, confirmatory testing requires:

1. A **preregistered mechanism/strategy** (not "the number was high"), and
2. **Genuinely uninspected evidence** — the sealed 2025 season — or an
   appropriately isolated chronological confirmation set.

The 2022–2024 FBSvFCS slice is BURNED for confirmation. It may inform the
mechanism but never the test statistic.

## Candidate mechanisms (to be preregistered, not asserted)

- M1: Motivation/effort asymmetry — FBS favorites run up scores vs FCS.
- M2: Market shading — books shade toward FCS underdogs (public dogs),
  creating value on FBS favorites.
- M3: Talent-gap nonlinearity — standard power ratings understate
  FBS-vs-FCS gaps, mispricing large spreads.
- M4: Home-field interaction — the effect is home-specific, not just
  favorite-specific.

Phase H must pick (or combine) mechanisms BEFORE touching 2025.

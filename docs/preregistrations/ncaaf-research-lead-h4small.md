**Date registered:** 2026-09-15.
**Status:** HYPOTHESIS REGISTRY. No confirmatory claim. No 2025 access.

## Observation (discovered post hoc on 2022–24)
The home underdog cover lean appears concentrated at spread magnitude
[0,3), potentially consistent with overpricing of home-field advantage
in near-pick'em games.

**2022–24 descriptive:**
- [0,3): dog − fav = +8.0pp (fav 42.4%, dog 50.4%)
- [3,7): +6.5pp
- [7,14): +4.2pp
- [14,∞): −1.1pp

## What is NOT established
- The causal mechanism. "HFA overpricing" is one possible explanation,
  not something the evidence has established.
- Whether the pattern replicates on any fresh data.
- Whether it is economically exploitable.

## Why 2022–24 cannot confirm it
The pattern was discovered by inspecting 2022–24 after seeing the H4
results. Those observations are burned for confirmatory purposes.

## Path forward (if pursued)
H4-small may become a preregistered hypothesis family tested on
genuinely new prospective observations (2026 shadow or later). It does
NOT get access to the sealed 2025 holdout — that is reserved for H5's
one-shot confirmation.

If developed, the hypothesis must specify BEFORE seeing new data:
- Exact spread bucket definition
- Exact window and consensus construction
- The mechanism being tested (not just the pattern)
- Statistical test and economic bar
